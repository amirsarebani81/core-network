=== Online Charging

==== Technical Overview

In online charging, the PCEF obtains credit from the Online Charging
System (OCS) before it lets the traffic of a service pass, and it reports
the used credit while the service continues. The PCEF uses Session
Charging with Unit Reservation (SCUR) on Gy: one Diameter Credit-Control
session carries an initial request, any number of update requests, and a
termination request, and each of them can report used units and request
new ones (TS 32.299 clause 6.3.5 and TS 32.251 clause 5.3.1.0). The
<<_pcc_charging_models>> page describes the quota management elements, and
the <<_pcc_gy_gz>> page describes the Gy reference point.

The figure below shows one rating group of a credit-control session in
the EPC with Control and User Plane Separation (CUPS), from the grant of a
quota to the redirection of the traffic after the final units are used.
The credit-control session already exists, because the PGW-C started it at
IP-CAN session establishment, as <<_pcc_ip_can_session_establishment>>
describes. The PGW-C decides on the thresholds of the Usage Reporting
Rules (URRs) in the PGW-U based on the quota that the OCS grants, so the
PGW-U reports the usage when the PGW-C needs to contact the OCS (TS 23.214
clause 5.3.2).

==== Call Flow

The call flow has three phases: Quota Grant, Quota Threshold Reached, and
Final Units Consumed.

==== Step-by-Step Description

. The PGW-C detects the start of a service data flow for which no valid
quota exists, so the *PGW-C* sends a `Credit-Control-Request` with the
`CC-Request-Type` set to `UPDATE_REQUEST` to the *OCS*. The CCR contains a
`Multiple-Services-Credit-Control` AVP with the `Rating-Group` of the
service, that is the charging key of its PCC rule, and can request units
in the `Requested-Service-Unit` AVP (TS 32.251 clauses 5.3.1.2 and
5.3.2.2.0, and TS 32.299 clause 6.3.5, step 6).

. The *OCS* reserves credit on the account of the subscriber and returns a
`Credit-Control-Answer` to the *PGW-C*. Its `Multiple-Services-Credit-Control`
AVP grants a quota in the `Granted-Service-Unit` AVP, for example a volume,
and can limit the validity of the quota in the `Validity-Time` AVP and set a
`Volume-Quota-Threshold` at which the PGW-C requests more units (TS 32.299
clauses 6.3.5, step 8, and 6.5.2, and IETF RFC 8506 section 8.33).

. The *PGW-C* sends a PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*.
The request provides a URR for the rating group with a volume threshold
and a volume quota that the PGW-C derives from the grant, and sets the
reporting triggers for the threshold (VOLTH) and the quota (VOLQU) (TS
23.214 clause 5.3.2 and TS 29.244 clause 5.2.2.2).

. The *PGW-U* confirms the update with a PFCP `Session Modification
Response` and starts to measure the traffic of the rating group.

. When the measured volume reaches the threshold, the *PGW-U* sends a PFCP
`Session Report Request` to the *PGW-C*, which carries a usage report with
the measured volume and the trigger that caused it (TS 29.244 clause
5.2.2.3.1).

. The *PGW-C* acknowledges the report with a PFCP `Session Report
Response`. The PGW-U continues to forward the traffic of the rating group
within the remaining quota (TS 29.244 clause 5.2.2.2).

. The *PGW-C* requests a re-authorization from the *OCS* with a
`Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type` set to
`UPDATE_REQUEST`. The CCR reports the used units in the
`Used-Service-Unit` AVP, with the `Reporting-Reason` AVP set to
`THRESHOLD` (0) (TS 32.299 clauses 6.5.1.3, 6.5.2, and 7.2.175). The PGW-C
lets the service continue while the re-authorization is in progress, until
the original quota is used up (TS 32.299 clause 6.5.2).

. The *OCS* deducts the reported units from the account and returns a
`Credit-Control-Answer`. In this example, the account can cover only a last
amount of units, so the OCS grants them as the final units: the
`Multiple-Services-Credit-Control` AVP contains a `Final-Unit-Indication`
AVP with the `Final-Unit-Action` set to `REDIRECT` and a `Redirect-Server`
AVP with the address of a top-up server (TS 32.299 clause 6.5.3 and IETF
RFC 8506 sections 8.34 to 8.37).

. The *PGW-C* sends a PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*
with the new quota for the URR. For the redirection, the PGW-C provides the
redirect destination to the PGW-U, or instructs the PGW-U to forward the
affected traffic to the PGW-C, depending on where the redirection is
enforced (TS 23.214 clause 5.11.3).

. The *PGW-U* confirms the update with a PFCP `Session Modification
Response`.

. When the final quota is used up, the *PGW-U* sends a PFCP `Session Report
Request` with a usage report for the volume quota trigger. From this point
the PGW-U applies the quota action that the PGW-C provisioned for the URR,
for example the redirection of the traffic (TS 29.244 clause 5.2.2.3.1).

. The *PGW-C* acknowledges the report with a PFCP `Session Report
Response`.

. The *PGW-C* starts the redirection of the service and informs the *OCS*
with a `Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type` set to
`UPDATE_REQUEST`. The CCR reports the used final units, with the
`Reporting-Reason` set to `QUOTA_EXHAUSTED` (3), and carries no
rating-related AVPs, which indicates that the final unit action has started
and that the OCS does not need to reserve more credit (IETF RFC 8506
section 5.6.2 and TS 32.299 clause 7.2.175).

. The *OCS* deducts the used units and answers with a
`Credit-Control-Answer` without a new reservation. The OCS can use the
`Validity-Time` AVP to limit how long the redirection lasts before the
PGW-C sends a new request (IETF RFC 8506 sections 5.6.2 and 8.33).

. If the PCRF armed the `OUT_OF_CREDIT` event trigger, the *PGW-C* informs
the *PCRF* with a Gx `Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type`
set to `UPDATE_REQUEST`. The CCR contains the `Event-Trigger`
`OUT_OF_CREDIT` (15) and a `Charging-Rule-Report` for the affected PCC
rules, which carries the `Final-Unit-Indication` of the termination action
that the PCEF applies (TS 23.203 clause 6.1.3 and TS 29.212 clauses 5.3.7
and 5.3.18).

. The *PCRF* acknowledges the report with a Gx `Credit-Control-Answer`,
which can also carry new or removed PCC rules for the affected services (TS
29.212 clause 5.6.3).

==== Termination Action TERMINATE

If the OCS sets the `Final-Unit-Action` to `TERMINATE` instead, the PCEF
terminates the service when the final units are used, or immediately when
the OCS grants a zero quota. It then reports the used units for the
terminated service in a CCR with the `CC-Request-Type` set to
`UPDATE_REQUEST` (TS 32.299 clause 6.5.3). `TERMINATE` is also the default
when the PCEF receives a `Final-Unit-Action` value that it does not support
(IETF RFC 8506 section 8.35). If the OCS has not provided any termination
action before it denies credit, the PCEF applies its preconfigured default
termination action (TS 23.203 clause 6.1.8).

==== Differences for GPRS

In GPRS, the GGSN runs one credit-control session for each PDP context,
and both measures and enforces the quota itself, so there are no PFCP
steps (TS 23.203 clause A.1.3.1.3). For a network-requested dedicated
bearer, the GGSN sends the initial request for the new PDP context to the
OCS before it starts any signaling towards the UE (TS 32.251 clause
5.3.2.2.1).
