=== IP-CAN Session Modification, PCRF-Initiated

==== Technical Overview

The PCRF starts an IP Connectivity Access Network (IP-CAN) Session
Modification when its own inputs change. These inputs include new or
modified service information from an Application Function (AF), a change
of the status of a policy counter that the Online Charging System (OCS)
reports, a change of the subscription information, and an internal
trigger such as a change of the configured policy (TS 23.203 clause 7.4.2
and TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.1). The PCRF provisions the new decision to the
PCEF without a request, which is the PUSH procedure of Gx (TS 29.212 clause
4.5.2.0).

The figure below shows the procedure for the EPC with Control and User
Plane Separation (CUPS) in the non-roaming case, with the example of a
Voice over LTE (VoLTE) call. The P-CSCF, acting as the AF, provides the
service information of the voice media over Rx, and the PCRF installs a PCC
rule with the QoS Class Identifier (QCI) 1, which TS 23.203 Table 6.1.7-A
standardizes as a GBR QCI for conversational voice. Because no existing
bearer has this QCI and ARP, the PGW-C establishes a dedicated bearer (TS
23.203 clause 6.1.1.4).

==== Call Flow

The call flow has five phases: Rx Service Information, Gx PCC Rule
Provisioning, Gy Credit Request, Dedicated Bearer Activation, and Resource
Allocation Report.

==== Step-by-Step Description

. The *AF* sends a Diameter `AA-Request` (AAR) over Rx to the *PCRF* to open
a new Rx session for the AF session. The AAR carries the UE IP address in
the `Framed-IP-Address` or `Framed-IPv6-Prefix` AVP and the service
information in `Media-Component-Description` AVPs, which for a voice call
contain the `Media-Type` `AUDIO`, the IP flows of the media and of RTCP in
`Media-Sub-Component` AVPs, and the requested bandwidth (TS 29.214 clauses
4.4.1 and 5.3.16, and TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.2.1.1, step 2). The AAR can
also carry the `AF-Charging-Identifier` AVP for charging correlation and
`Specific-Action` AVPs that subscribe the AF to bearer events, for example
`INDICATION_OF_SUCCESSFUL_RESOURCES_ALLOCATION` and
`INDICATION_OF_LOSS_OF_BEARER` (TS 29.214 clauses 4.4.1 and 5.3.13).

. The *PCRF* stores the service information, identifies the affected
IP-CAN session, and confirms the request with a Diameter `AA-Answer` (AAA)
to the *AF* (TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.2.1.1, steps 3, 6, and 7). If the PCRF
does not have the subscription information of the user, it first retrieves
it from the SPR (TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.2.1.1, steps 4 and 5).

. The *PCRF* binds the AF session to the IP-CAN session, derives the
authorized QoS of the media from the service information, and sends a
Diameter `Re-Auth-Request` (RAR) over Gx to the *PGW-C* (TS 23.203 clauses
6.1.1.2 and 6.1.1.3, and TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.1, step 6). The RAR carries
a `Charging-Rule-Install` AVP with a `Charging-Rule-Definition` for each
media flow, which contains the `Flow-Information` filters, the
`Flow-Status`, the `QoS-Information` with the QCI, the ARP, and the
guaranteed and maximum bitrates, and the charging parameters (TS 29.212
clause 5.3.4). To receive a confirmation of the bearer establishment, the
PCRF sets the `Event-Trigger` `SUCCESSFUL_RESOURCE_ALLOCATION` (22) and
includes the `Resource-Allocation-Notification` AVP with the value
`ENABLE_NOTIFICATION` (0) in the `Charging-Rule-Install` AVP (TS 29.212
clause 4.5.2.0).

. The *PGW-C* installs the PCC rules and acknowledges the RAR with a
Diameter `Re-Auth-Answer` (RAA) to the *PCRF* (TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.1,
steps 7 and 8). A successful RAA means that the PCC rules are installed,
but not that the bearer binding or the resource reservation is complete
(TS 29.212 clause 4.5.2.0, NOTE 4).

. If online charging applies and no valid quota exists for the charging
key of the new PCC rules, the *PGW-C* requests credit for that charging key
from the *OCS* with a `Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type`
set to `UPDATE_REQUEST` over Gy (TS 23.203 clause 7.4.2, step 14, and TS
32.251 clause 5.3.2.2.2).

. The *OCS* returns a `Credit-Control-Answer` with a quota for the new
rating group (TS 23.203 clause 7.4.2, step 15).

. The bearer binding finds no existing bearer with the QCI and the ARP of
the new PCC rules, so the *PGW-C* initiates a dedicated bearer. It sends a
PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*, which asks the PGW-U to
allocate an F-TEID for the new bearer (TS 23.203 clause 6.1.1.4 and TS
23.214 clause 6.3.1.7, step 2).

. The *PGW-U* returns a PFCP `Session Modification Response` with the
allocated F-TEID (TS 23.214 clause 6.3.1.7, step 3).

. The *PGW-C* sends a GTPv2-C `Create Bearer Request` to the *SGW-C*, with
the Bearer TFT that it derives from the filters of the PCC rules and the
Bearer Level QoS of the new bearer (TS 23.401 clause 5.4.1, step 2). The
SGW-C, the MME, the eNodeB, and the UE then establish the bearer, as
<<_dedicated_bearer_activation_procedure>> describes.

. The *SGW-C* returns a GTPv2-C `Create Bearer Response` to the *PGW-C*,
which confirms the activation of the bearer (TS 23.401 clause 5.4.1, step
11).

. The *PGW-C* sends a PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*
with the F-TEID of the activated bearer that it received from the SGW-C
(TS 23.214 clause 6.3.1.7, step 11).

. The *PGW-U* confirms the update with a PFCP `Session Modification
Response` (TS 23.214 clause 6.3.1.7, step 12).

. Because the PCRF requested a confirmation of the resource allocation,
the *PGW-C* starts a PCEF-initiated IP-CAN Session Modification and sends a
`Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type` set to `UPDATE_REQUEST`
to the *PCRF*. The CCR carries the `Event-Trigger`
`SUCCESSFUL_RESOURCE_ALLOCATION` and a `Charging-Rule-Report` with the
`PCC-Rule-Status` set to `ACTIVE` for the installed rules (TS 29.213 clause
4.3.2.1, step 18, and TS 29.212 clauses 4.5.2.0 and 5.3.19).

. The *PCRF* acknowledges the report with a `Credit-Control-Answer` to the
*PGW-C* (TS 29.213 clause 4.3.2.1, step 13).

. If the AF subscribed to the event, the *PCRF* sends a Diameter `RAR` over
Rx to the *AF* with the `Specific-Action` AVP set to
`INDICATION_OF_SUCCESSFUL_RESOURCES_ALLOCATION` (8) and the affected flows
in the `Flows` AVP (TS 29.214 clause 5.3.13).

. The *AF* acknowledges the notification with a Diameter `RAA`.

==== Differences for GPRS

In GPRS, the PCRF-initiated modification can lead to a GGSN-initiated PDP
context modification or deactivation, or to a network-requested secondary
PDP context activation when the bearer binding in the GGSN requires a new
PDP context (TS 23.203 clause A.1.4.4.2). When the selected bearer control
mode is UE-only, or when the UE mode applies to the affected PCC rules in
UE/NW mode, the PCRF can instead wait for the UE to request the bearer
resources, and then provides the PCC rules in the answer to the resulting
PCEF-initiated request (TS 29.213 clause 4.3.1.1, step 4). When the PCRF
performs the bearer binding, it also includes the bearer identifier in the
`Charging-Rule-Install` AVP (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.2).

==== Possible Failures and Required Actions

Service information rejected:: The PCRF can reject service information that
is not consistent with the subscription information or the operator
policy, and it can indicate in the AAA the service information that it
would accept (TS 23.203 clause 6.2.1.0 and TS 29.214 clause 5.3.16).

Bearer establishment fails:: If the PCEF cannot establish the dedicated
bearer, it removes the affected PCC rules and reports them to the PCRF in a
CCR with the `CC-Request-Type` set to `UPDATE_REQUEST`, a
`Charging-Rule-Report` with the `PCC-Rule-Status` set to `INACTIVE`, and the
`Rule-Failure-Code` set to `RESOURCE_ALLOCATION_FAILURE` (10) (TS 29.212
clause 4.5.6). The PCRF then informs the AF if the AF subscribed to such
events (TS 29.214 clause 4.4.6.2).
