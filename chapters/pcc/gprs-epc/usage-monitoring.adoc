=== Usage Monitoring

==== Technical Overview

Usage monitoring lets the PCRF base its policy decisions on the total
network usage of a user in real time, independently of the charging of
that user. The PCRF provides volume or time thresholds for a monitoring
key, and the PCEF reports the accumulated usage when a threshold is
reached. The PCRF can then, for example, reduce the authorized bandwidth
of the user (TS 23.203 clauses 4.4 and 6.6). <<_pcc_policy_triggers_and_usage_monitoring>>
describes the monitoring keys, the thresholds, and the conditions for
reporting.

The figure below shows the procedure for the EPC with Control and User
Plane Separation (CUPS) in the non-roaming case, with one example: the
PCRF activates the monitoring of all the traffic of the IP-CAN session,
and when the volume threshold is reached, it reduces the APN-AMBR of the
user. The PGW-C measures the usage through Usage Reporting Rules (URRs)
that it provisions in the PGW-U for each monitoring key (TS 23.214 clause
5.3.2).

==== Call Flow

The call flow has three phases: Usage Monitoring Activation, Usage
Threshold Reached, and Enforcement of the New Policy.

==== Step-by-Step Description

. The *PCRF* sends a Diameter `Re-Auth-Request` over Gx to the *PGW-C*
that activates usage monitoring. The RAR sets the `Event-Trigger` to
`USAGE_REPORT` (33) and contains a `Usage-Monitoring-Information` AVP with
the `Monitoring-Key`, the volume threshold in the `CC-Total-Octets` AVP of
the `Granted-Service-Unit` AVP, and the `Usage-Monitoring-Level` AVP set to
`SESSION_LEVEL` (0) for the whole IP-CAN session (TS 29.212 clauses 4.5.16,
5.3.60, and 5.3.61). The PCRF can provide the same information in the CCA
of the IP-CAN session establishment instead (TS 29.213 clause 4.1, step
13).

. The *PGW-C* acknowledges the RAR with a Diameter `Re-Auth-Answer`.

. The *PGW-C* sends a PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*
with a URR for the monitoring key, whose volume threshold the PGW-C
derives from the threshold that the PCRF provided (TS 23.214 clause 5.3.2
and TS 29.244 clause 5.2.2.2).

. The *PGW-U* confirms the update with a PFCP `Session Modification
Response` and starts to measure the traffic of the IP-CAN session.

. When the measured volume reaches the threshold, the *PGW-U* sends a PFCP
`Session Report Request` with a usage report for the URR to the *PGW-C*
(TS 23.214 clause 5.3.3 and TS 29.244 clause 5.2.2.3.1).

. The *PGW-C* acknowledges the report with a PFCP `Session Report
Response`. The PGW-U continues to measure the usage after the threshold is
reached, until the PCRF provides a new threshold (TS 23.203 clause
6.2.2.3).

. The *PGW-C* reports the usage to the *PCRF* in a Diameter
`Credit-Control-Request` with the `CC-Request-Type` set to
`UPDATE_REQUEST` and the `Event-Trigger` set to `USAGE_REPORT`. The
`Usage-Monitoring-Information` AVP contains the `Monitoring-Key` and the
usage accumulated since the last report in the `Used-Service-Unit` AVP (TS
29.212 clauses 4.5.17.1 and 5.3.60). If both volume and time thresholds
were provided, the PGW-C reports both measurements even when only one
threshold is reached (TS 23.203 clause 6.1.4).

. The *PCRF* makes a new policy decision based on the usage and returns a
`Credit-Control-Answer` to the *PGW-C*. In this example, the CCA carries a
reduced authorized APN-AMBR in the `QoS-Information` AVP. To continue the
monitoring, the CCA also carries a new threshold in the
`Usage-Monitoring-Information` AVP; without a new threshold, the PGW-C
stops monitoring that key (TS 29.212 clause 4.5.16 and TS 23.203 clauses
6.2.2.3 and A.4.4.4.1).

. The *PGW-C* sends a PFCP `Session Modification Request` to the *PGW-U*
with the new APN-AMBR for policing and the new threshold for the URR (TS
23.214 clauses 5.10 and 6.3.1.7, step 11).

. The *PGW-U* confirms the update with a PFCP `Session Modification
Response` (TS 23.214 clause 6.3.1.7, step 12).

. Because the APN-AMBR changed, the *PGW-C* starts the PDN GW initiated
bearer modification without bearer QoS update and sends a GTPv2-C `Update
Bearer Request` with the new APN-AMBR to the *SGW-C* (TS 23.401 clause
5.4.3, step 2). The SGW-C, the MME, and the UE then apply the new APN-AMBR.

. The *SGW-C* returns a GTPv2-C `Update Bearer Response` to the *PGW-C*
(TS 23.401 clause 5.4.3).

==== Monitoring Time

The PCRF can also provide a monitoring time, for example the start of a
new billing day, together with subsequent thresholds. At the monitoring
time, the PCEF records the accumulated usage and applies the subsequent
thresholds, and its next usage report separates the usage before and after
the monitoring time (TS 23.203 clauses 6.2.2.3 and 6.6.1, and TS 29.212
clause 4.5.16).

==== Differences for GPRS

In GPRS, the GGSN performs the measurement itself, so there are no PFCP
steps. The PCRF can provide a new authorized APN-AMBR in the CCA of the
IP-CAN session modification, and the GGSN enforces it for the total
bandwidth of the non-GBR traffic of the APN (TS 23.203 clauses A.1.3.2.2.1
and A.1.4.4.1).
