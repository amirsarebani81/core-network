=== Gx Application

==== Technical Overview

The Gx reference point connects the Policy and Charging Enforcement
Function (PCEF) and the Policy and Charging Rules Function (PCRF), and
lets the PCRF control the PCC behavior of the PCEF dynamically (TS 23.203
clause 5.2.2). In GPRS the PCEF is the GGSN, and in the EPC with Control
and User Plane Separation (CUPS) it is the PGW-C.

Gx is a vendor-specific Diameter application with the Application-ID
16777238 and the 3GPP Vendor-ID 10415. The PCRF acts as the Diameter
server, because it handles the requests for PCC rules within a realm, and
the PCEF acts as the Diameter client (TS 29.212 clause 5.1). Gx reuses
commands of the Diameter base protocol and of the Diameter Credit-Control
application, and adds its own AVPs to them (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.1). The
<<_diameter,Diameter>> chapter describes the base protocol and the
Credit-Control application.

The PCEF establishes one Diameter session, the Gx session, for each IP
Connectivity Access Network (IP-CAN) session. For an IP-CAN type with
several bearers, such as GPRS, the PCEF establishes the Gx session with the
first IP-CAN bearer of the IP-CAN session (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.1). The
PCEF can also skip the Gx session for an IP-CAN session when the Charging
Characteristics from the HSS ask it to (TS 29.212 clause 4.5.1).

==== Provisioning Modes

The PCRF provides PCC rules to the PCEF in one of two ways (TS 29.212
clause 4.5.2.0):

PULL procedure:: The PCEF requests PCC rules in a `CC-Request` (CCR), and
the PCRF provides them in the `CC-Answer` (CCA). The PCEF requests rules
at IP-CAN session establishment, at IP-CAN session modification, and when
an event trigger is met (TS 29.212 clause 4.5.1).

PUSH procedure:: The PCRF provides PCC rules without a request, in a
`Re-Auth-Request` (RAR), for example after it receives service information
from an Application Function (AF) over Rx. The RAR triggers no CCR/CCA
exchange, and the PCRF does not usually send another RAR for the same
IP-CAN session until the PCEF acknowledges the previous one.

==== Gx Commands

[cols="2,1,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Command | Code | Sender | Use

| `CC-Request` (CCR)
| 272
| PCEF
| Requests PCC rules, reports events, the status of PCC rules, and accumulated usage, and indicates the termination of the IP-CAN session (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.2).

| `CC-Answer` (CCA)
| 272
| PCRF
| Provides PCC rules, event triggers, the authorized QoS, the bearer control mode, and the usage monitoring thresholds in response to a CCR (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.3).

| `Re-Auth-Request` (RAR)
| 258
| PCRF
| Provisions PCC rules, event triggers, and event report indications unsolicited, or requests the termination of the IP-CAN session (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.4).

| `Re-Auth-Answer` (RAA)
| 258
| PCEF
| Acknowledges an RAR, and can report the status of the PCC rules and access network information (TS 29.212 clause 5.6.5).
|===

The `CC-Request-Type` AVP of each CCR marks its role in the Gx session:

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| `CC-Request-Type` | Use on Gx

| `INITIAL_REQUEST` (1)
| The PCEF establishes the Gx session at IP-CAN session establishment, and supplies the UE identity, the IP-CAN type, the RAT type, the PDN information, the UE IP address, and further session information (TS 29.212 clause 4.5.1).

| `UPDATE_REQUEST` (2)
| The PCEF requests PCC rules at IP-CAN session modification, for example when an event trigger is met, and reports the status of PCC rules or accumulated usage (TS 29.212 clauses 4.5.1 and 4.5.17.1).

| `TERMINATION_REQUEST` (3)
| The PCEF indicates that the IP-CAN session is being terminated, and reports the final accumulated usage (TS 29.212 clauses 4.5.7 and 4.5.17.4).
|===

==== Key AVPs

The table below describes the Gx AVPs that the procedures of this chapter
use most. TS 29.212 Table 5.3.0.1 lists all the Gx specific AVPs.

[cols="2,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| AVP | Code | Purpose

| `Charging-Rule-Install`
| 1001
| Activates, installs, or modifies PCC rules. It carries `Charging-Rule-Definition` AVPs for dynamic rules, and `Charging-Rule-Name` or `Charging-Rule-Base-Name` AVPs for predefined rules or groups of predefined rules (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.2).

| `Charging-Rule-Remove`
| 1002
| Removes dynamic PCC rules, or deactivates predefined PCC rules or groups of rules, by name (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.3).

| `Charging-Rule-Definition`
| 1003
| Defines a dynamic PCC rule. It carries the `Charging-Rule-Name`, which identifies the rule within the IP-CAN session, and for example the `Flow-Information` filters or a `TDF-Application-Identifier`, the `Flow-Status` gate, the `QoS-Information`, the `Rating-Group`, the `Service-Identifier`, the `Online`, `Offline`, `Metering-Method`, and `Reporting-Level` charging parameters, the `Precedence`, and the `Monitoring-Key` (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.4).

| `Charging-Rule-Report`
| 1018
| Reports the status of PCC rules in the `PCC-Rule-Status` AVP, which is `ACTIVE` (0), `INACTIVE` (1), or `TEMPORARILY INACTIVE` (2), together with a `Rule-Failure-Code` when a rule cannot be installed or enforced (TS 29.212 clauses 5.3.18, 5.3.19, and 5.3.38).

| `QoS-Information`
| 1016
| Carries the QoS of an IP-CAN bearer, a PCC rule, a QCI, or an APN: the `QoS-Class-Identifier`, the maximum and guaranteed bitrates, the `Allocation-Retention-Priority`, and the APN-AMBR. From the PCEF it carries the requested QoS, and from the PCRF the authorized QoS (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.16).

| `Default-EPS-Bearer-QoS`
| 1049
| Carries the QCI and the ARP of the default EPS bearer, which the PCEF reports and the PCRF authorizes (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.48).

| `Event-Trigger`
| 1006
| From the PCRF, subscribes to events. From the PCEF, reports the event that caused the request (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.7).

| `Usage-Monitoring-Information`
| 1067
| Carries the `Monitoring-Key` and the thresholds or the accumulated usage of one usage monitoring instance (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.60).

| `Bearer-Control-Mode`
| 1023
| Carries the bearer control mode that the PCRF selected, `UE_ONLY` (0) or `UE_NW` (2) (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.23).

| `IP-CAN-Type`
| 1027
| Identifies the type of the IP-CAN, for example `3GPP-GPRS` (0) for an access through a GGSN over Gn/Gp, or `3GPP-EPS` (5) (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.27).

| `RAT-Type`
| 1032
| Identifies the radio access technology that serves the UE, for example `UTRAN` (1000), `GERAN` (1001), or `EUTRAN` (1004) (TS 29.212 clause 5.3.31).
|===

When the PCRF modifies a dynamic PCC rule, the optional AVPs that it
omits from the `Charging-Rule-Definition` keep their previous values, but
new `Flow-Information` AVPs replace all the previous ones (TS 29.212 clause
5.3.4). In the same way, a `QoS-Information` AVP that the PCRF provided
earlier stays valid when a later message omits it (TS 29.212 clause
5.3.16).

==== Result Codes

Gx uses the `Result-Code` values of the Diameter base protocol, for example
`DIAMETER_SUCCESS` (2001), and reports Gx specific results in the
`Experimental-Result` AVP with the Vendor-ID 10415 (TS 29.212 clauses 5.5.1
and 5.5.2). Examples of Gx specific permanent failures are:

`DIAMETER_ERROR_INITIAL_PARAMETERS` (5140):: The information that the PCRF
needs to select rules, for example the QoS, the RAT type, or the
subscriber information, is incomplete, erroneous, or not available.

`DIAMETER_ERROR_TRIGGER_EVENT` (5141):: The information in a CCR that an
event trigger caused is not coherent with the previous information, for
example a RAT change to the same RAT.

`DIAMETER_PCC_RULE_EVENT` (5142):: The PCEF cannot install or activate the
PCC rules, and it identifies the affected rules in the
`Charging-Rule-Report` AVP.

These definitions come from TS 29.212 clause 5.5.3.
