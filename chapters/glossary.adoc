= Glossary

This glossary lists the abbreviations that this book uses, together with
their expanded forms. The text spells out each abbreviation at its first
use in each part and uses only the abbreviation after that, so a reader
who starts in the middle of a part can look up an abbreviation here.
3GPP TR 21.905 defines the common vocabulary of the 3GPP specifications,
and the specification that each chapter cites defines the abbreviations
that are specific to one protocol or procedure.
